  Triton 2-D multi_consumer_cat_3kind_2d_kernel -- nsys timing sweep
  Experiment A1: (OperationName, ResultType) dedup cache with 3
                 DISTINCT consumer kinds on a single fp32 tt.cat.
  Reducers: arith.fptosi (i8), arith.truncf (fp16), tt.fp_to_fp (fp8e5).
  Cache must produce 3 independent rewritten chains -- no false sharing.
