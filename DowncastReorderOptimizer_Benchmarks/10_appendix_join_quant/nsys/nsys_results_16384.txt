Comparison vs 8192^2 results (from nsys_results_2d.txt)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  At 8192^2, the only meaningful win was at (256, 256): 1.11x
  All other tile shapes were essentially flat between OFF and ON.
  16384^2 quadruples the launch work; relative speedups can change
  if a tile that was below the spill cliff at 8192 crosses it at 16384.
