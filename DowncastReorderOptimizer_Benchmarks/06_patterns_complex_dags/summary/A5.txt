--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT A5 TESTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A5 is one of the seven DAG-topology gaps in future_experiments.txt.
It tests that runOnce's worklist can rewrite a chain of THREE nested
cats from a SINGLE seed (the outer cat) in ONE sweep. If the worklist
failed to re-queue upstream movers correctly, the outer driver's
defensive 16-iteration fixed-point loop would have to clean up.

Kernel pattern (cat-of-cats with 4 inputs):

   load a (fp32)  load b   load c   load d
        \\        /          \\      /
         cat_ab               cat_cd       <- inner cats
            \\               /
              ____ cat_abcd ___              <- outer cat
                       |
                    truncf-fp16
                       |
                    reshape
                       |
                    store fp16


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WORKLIST TRACE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Initial walk seeds ONLY cat_abcd (its direct user is truncf-fp16,
a reducer). The inner cats (cat_ab, cat_cd) have non-reducer users
(the outer cat), so they are NOT initially eligible.

Pop cat_abcd:
  Push truncf above cat_abcd. cat_abcd becomes cat_abcd-on-fp16.
  Upstream re-queue: cat_ab, cat_cd (both safe movers feeding
  cat_abcd).

Pop cat_cd:
  Its user is now the cloned truncf from above. Rewrite. cat_cd
  becomes cat_cd-on-fp16. Upstream re-queue: the two upstream
  reshapes (which feed c_flat, d_flat).

Pop cat_ab:
  Same -- cat_ab-on-fp16. Upstream re-queue: the reshapes feeding
  a_flat, b_flat.

Pop and rewrite the four upstream reshapes (each on a fp32 load).

End state: 4 cloned truncfs (one per loaded input), 3 fp16 cats
(cat_ab, cat_cd, cat_abcd, all narrowed), 0 fp32 cats. All in ONE
runOnce call -- the outer 16-iteration cap is not engaged.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. STRUCTURAL PROOF (TTIR)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

OFF TTIR (relevant ops):
  %ab   = tt.cat %a_flat, %b_flat : f32 -> 32768xf32
  %cd   = tt.cat %c_flat, %d_flat : f32 -> 32768xf32
  %abcd = tt.cat %ab, %cd : 32768xf32 -> 65536xf32
  %e    = arith.truncf %abcd : f32 to f16

ON TTIR (relevant ops):
  %e    = arith.truncf %a_19 : 128x128 fp32 to fp16    (one of 4)
  %e_32 = arith.truncf %b_23 : 128x128 fp32 to fp16
  %e_33 = arith.truncf %c_27 : 128x128 fp32 to fp16
  %e_34 = arith.truncf %d_31 : 128x128 fp32 to fp16
  ... (each followed by its reshape to 16384xf16)
  %ab   = tt.cat %a_flat, %b_flat : 16384xf16 -> 32768xf16
  %cd   = tt.cat %c_flat, %d_flat : 16384xf16 -> 32768xf16
  %abcd = tt.cat %ab, %cd        : 32768xf16 -> 65536xf16

Single-sweep convergence VERIFIED: 5 worklist pops, 1 runOnce.

5. PERFORMANCE (10 nsys configs)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  M=N=8192:                            M=N=4096:
    tile     OFF us   ON us    sp      tile     OFF us  ON us    sp
    64x64    3514.7   3501.2   1.00x   64x64     926.8   923.6   1.00x
    64x256  18729.7   3291.4   5.69x   64x256   4625.1   838.6   5.52x
    128x128 18214.0   3298.6   5.52x   128x128  4476.5   862.3   5.19x
    128x256 18701.4  20954.9   0.89x   128x256  4557.6  5098.4   0.89x
    256x128 18234.3  19451.2   0.94x   256x128  4456.0  4753.3   0.94x

  Summary: 4 wins / 2 neutral / 4 regressions. Best 5.69x at 64x256.

A5 has the LARGEST SPEEDUP in the bundle (5.69x at 64x256) AND four
regressions. Mechanism: with 3 cats and 4 cloned truncfs after the
pass, the per-chain register pressure varies dramatically with tile
size. At 64x256 the four narrow chains fit comfortably; at 128x256
they collide and spill more than the single fp32 chain did.

6. NCU AT THE REGR (128x256, 8192) -- MEASUREMENT QUIRK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  metric        OFF         ON
  duration      18671us     18633us           (0.99x, neutral in ncu)
  DRAM total    9179 MB     9180 MB           (no change in single launch)
  spill LD      28.66 M     28.66 M           (no change)
  spill ST      29.38 M     29.38 M           (no change)
  regs/thread   64          64                (same regime)
  occupancy     94.3%       94.4%             (same)

NOTE: the ncu single-launch capture of A5 regr shows OFF and ON
essentially equal, while the nsys 100-launch average shows a 0.89x
regression. Possible causes:
  - ncu's --launch-skip 5 captures a single post-warmup launch which
    may not match the average behavior over 100 runs.
  - ncu's kernel replay (22 passes in the trace) measures a stable-
    state launch that differs from the average.

The PER-LAUNCH nsys variance (a 0.89x regression averaged over 100
launches) is the more reliable headline number. The single-launch
ncu telemetry confirms there is NO register-count or spill-count
change between OFF and ON at this tile -- so any regression is in
the kernel-launch path or in launch-to-launch variance, not in
steady-state per-launch work.

This refinement is useful for the future B8 register-pressure
heuristic: when steady-state metrics tie but the multi-launch
average regresses, look elsewhere.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. WHAT A5 ADDS TO THE STORY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Single-sweep convergence verified on a 3-cat nested mover chain
    starting from a single worklist seed.
  - LARGEST single-tile speedup in the entire study (5.69x at 64x256
    on 8192^2).
  - Shows the regression family from dual_quant_2d / A1 scales with
    cloned-chain count: 4 cloned chains regress at 0.89x; A1's 3
    cloned chains regress at 0.79x; dual_quant_2d's 2 cloned chains
    regress at 0.68x (different tile counts, same mechanism). Future
    B8 heuristic should consider chain-count when deciding to fire.
  - Documents a meaningful discrepancy between single-launch ncu
    and multi-launch nsys averages for the regression cases.
